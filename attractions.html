Key Attractions

Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque

The golden-domed landmark of Bandar Seri Begawan. Visit outside prayer times.

Kampong Ayer

A huge water village on stilts. Take a water taxi from the waterfront.

Ulu Temburong National Park

Rainforest with a canopy walkway above the trees. Reached by longboat.

Jerudong Park

A leisure park with gardens and rides. Check opening status first.

National Museums

The Royal Regalia and Malay Technology museums show Brunei's history.

Opening hours change. Please check to confirm on the official Brunei Tourism website
