Plan Your Visit

Getting there

Fly into Brunei International Airport (BWN), or travel by road from Miri in Sarawak, Malaysia. Check the Royal Brunei Airlines website to fly around the country

Getting around

Use taxis, ride-hailing apps or a rental car. Water taxis cross to Kampong Ayer.

Entry and visas

Rules depend on your nationality. Check the Immigration Department website before you travel.

Accommodation

Choose from hotels in Bandar Seri Begawan, guesthouses and homestays.

Best time to visit

It is hot and humid all year. March to September is usually drier and November to January is usually wetter.

Tip

Plan outdoor trips for the morning and carry a light rain jacket.

Ministry of Tourism, Brunei Darussalam
