Welcome to Brunei Darussalam

Brunei, "the Abode of Peace", is a small country on the island of Borneo in Southeast Asia. It is known for golden mosques, a village built on the river and some of the best-preserved rainforest in the region.

Reasons to visit: safe and friendly streets, rich Malay Islamic culture, delicious local food and easy access to nature.

Quick facts: Capital: Bandar Seri Begawan | Currency: Brunei dollar | Languages: Malay and English.
