Discover Brunei

Brunei sits on the north coast of Borneo which is facing the South China Sea. The country is split into two parts by the Malaysian state of Sarawak and the capital is Bandar Seri Begawan. It is a Malay Islamic monarchy, and its oil and gas wealth supports a calm, orderly way of life.

Culture and customs

Dress nicely

Cover your shoulders and knees, especially at mosques.

Shoes off

Remove your shoes before entering mosques and homes.

Use your right hand

Give, receive and eat with your right hand.

No alcohol sold

Alcohol is not sold, and shops often close around Friday midday prayers.

Local cuisine

Ambuyat

The national dish: sticky sago eaten with a bamboo fork and sauce.

Nasi katok

Rice, fried chicken and spicy sambal. Cheap and popular.

Gadong Night Market

Grilled snacks and sweet cakes in the evening.
