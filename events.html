Events and Festivals

Islamic holiday dates change every year, so check before you book.

23 February: National Day

Parades, flags and fireworks celebrate independence.

31 May: Armed Forces Day

Ceremonial displays and public events.

15 July: Sultan's Birthday

A major national celebration with processions.

Ramadan and Hari Raya

Evening food bazaars, then open houses where visitors may be invited to eat.
